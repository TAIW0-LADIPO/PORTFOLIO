Doctoral research · ongoing

LM-PAEK powder development for laser powder bed fusion

Investigating feedstock preparation and powder qualification for high-performance polymer additive manufacturing within the thermal constraints of a Farsoon STP 252 system.

Preliminary investigations cover sieving, ambient milling, particle morphology, particle size distribution, dynamic powder flow and thermal characterisation. Cryogenic milling, flow-agent evaluation and process validation are next steps.

Methods: optical microscopy, laser diffraction, GranuDrum, DSC and TGA.

Master's research · completed

High-temperature extrusion equipment retrofit

Adapted tabletop filament extrusion and FFF equipment for processing PEEK composites, integrating a direct-drive extruder, custom carriage, controller and firmware changes.

280 → 420 °C

Reported filament extruder temperature capability after modification; printer hot-end capability increased to 400 °C.
